10.2

Season 1

05.03-01.06.2026«Death Is the First Step»

Season 1, "Death Is the First Step", ran from March 5 to June 1, 2026. canon

What happened

The start of the "gold rush", in Bungie's own framing the opening of a proxy war between the factions for power and for answers about the 30,000 lost souls of New Cascadia. At launch there were three surface zones, 28 guns and six shells; a beta of the ranked mode followed at the end of March. The peak was the ARG (details in file 12): the community collectively opened the way to orbit, into the Cryo Archive. The season ended on June 1 with the first full progress wipe. Inside the world it is explained as your Runner's memory degrading between transfers. canon

Why the wipe reads as lore

Live-service games usually apologise for seasonal resets; Marathon's fiction turns them into tragedy. Memory degradation between mind transfers is confirmed canon, so a new season is your Runner waking up after another transfer, with holes where skills and gear used to be. The grounding ritual from file 05 is the onboarding for exactly that moment. Where lost loadouts physically go, Bungie hasn't explained in the story. Fans offer two versions: the gear stays on bodies left somewhere on Tau Ceti, or CyberAcme seizes it against the debt every Runner carries. Both rest on a theme the game does have (Runners are shown as CyberAcme's debtors), but the Codex states neither word for word. The seizure version works better as drama: you are a photocopy of a photocopy, and the original sits with the corporation. In practice a wipe takes your gear and progress while the knowledge stays: maps, enemy habits and instinct come with you into the new season. (practical guide by NULL024) theory (thanks to u/N1349m for the tip)

Oct 6The game confirmed the seizure version itself: in Nightfall Refresh ONI announced that Runners' credits and gear had gone to pay off their debt (see 10.7). canon

10.3

Nightfall

Season 2 // since 02 JUN 2026Escalation

The theme of the season is escalation. The UESC, enraged by constant raids, builds up its forces and hides its secrets ever more desperately. The noise wakes something else: the Anomaly is changing, and after sunset the planet gets frightening. Runners fight the UESC, and out in the dark sits a third party that cares about neither. canon

What's new, and the enemy in the dark

What the season added: the Sentinel shell, the Night Marsh zone, the Cradle progression system (you convert looted gear into energy and invest it in permanent stats), two guns, the KKV-9SD and the D54 Battle Pistol (the season had no other new weapons), implants reworked into fixed perk packs, and an experimental PvP-lite mode, "Sponsored Survival". Separately, Bungie announced a new enemy in the dark that Runners had never met before. Kotaku's Ethan Gach described the silhouette in the trailer as something between a Xenomorph and Slender Man. Fan guesses range from Runner mimics to fungal hosts from the Codex entries on Class B anomalous material. Who it is was never revealed. theory unrevealed

How it ended

Season 2 was never followed by a third: events, the September 24 roadmap and Nightfall Refresh came instead. The Nightfall name stayed with the October 6 update. canon

10.4

Vault Breaker

Update 1.1.5 // 21 JUL 2026Mid-season

Mid-season update 1.1.5 (July 21-22, 2026) brought Vault Breaker, the game's first experimental PvE mode: solo players, duos or trios work through a chain of Cryo Archive vaults, and the loot stays behind at the door. canon

Mode windows

The first window, July 21 - August 4, was extended to August 18 by patch 1.1.5.2. The second window, October 20 - November 9, 2026, was announced together with Nightfall Refresh. canon

Cradle Evolution

The same update brought Cradle Evolution: a voluntary full reset of the Cradle tree from Runner level 70+, up to seven tiers, each giving +1 to maximum energy, plus exclusive cosmetics. canon

Patches in the window

1.1.5.xOn July 24 a hotfix cut the V22 Volt Thrower from 18 to 12.6. On July 28 (1.1.5.1) VIP reward packages arrived. On August 4 (1.1.5.2) the Volt Thrower was partly restored to 14.4, with projectile tracking fading to 30% over 1.2 s; the KKV-9SD base magazine went from 23 to 28; free Sponsored Kits got stronger. On August 11 (1.1.5.3) an FPS counter was added. canon

The large 1.1.5 balance pass shifted the weapon meta, so 06-B // Arsenal covers each gun's role and lore and leaves live numbers out. Full notes: Bungie's feed. canon

Ordnance Heist

Update 1.1.5.4 // 18 AUG - 01 SEP 2026The Firestorm hunt

The second limited event, announced on August 17: a multi-run PvPvE hunt for the Firestorm, the game's first power weapon, with three unique mods. canon

  1. 01In Perimeter you farm the Key Template and UESC Manifests.
  2. 02You complete the template task on Dire Marsh or Night Marsh.
  3. 03You get the Firestorm Locker Key.
  4. 04With three keys you open a locker in Perimeter, Dire Marsh or Outpost.

The route was pieced together by community guides. datamine Manifests were spent at the MIDA and Traxus armories. canon

Patches in the window

1.1.5.4The same patch nerfed the V85 Circuit Breaker (the available notes give no exact numbers) and added more loot to Ranked. canon

1.1.5.5August 25: shared Manifests in a squad were fixed (+150 arrived by mail), a template now gives 3 keys instead of 1, and Firestorm locker loot was improved. canon

1.1.9On September 1 the C.A.R.R.I armory opened at CyberAcme (details in file 07); on September 15 patch 1.1.9.1 fixed a bug that sold off the whole Overflow. canon

On July 16 Bungie announced Season 3 for September 22: a Perimeter rework, a new shell and full PvE. It never shipped in that form. On September 24, in the post "The Future of Marathon", the studio dropped the fixed three-month season cycle. canon

The new plan

Nightfall Refresh on October 6, Symbiosis on December 8 and a major update in March 2027. Bungie wrote plainly that the game "did not reach as many players as we hoped", and design lead Kevin Yanes named the "constant loop of failure" for new players as a problem. canon

Context

On July 17 game director Joe Ziegler left Bungie; the game is now led by new game director Del Chafe III and creative director Julia Nardin. Over the summer the studio went through mass layoffs. Reviews at launch were mostly positive, singling out the gunplay, the art direction and the lore. canon

We don't summarise the September 17 post "The Next Chapter for Bungie": its text hasn't been checked yet.

10.7

Refresh

Update 1.1.9.2 // 06.10.2026Nightfall Refresh

On October 6 (patch 1.033 on consoles) progress was wiped for everyone: Runner, Cradle and faction levels, Priority Contracts, Rank, Vault and zone access. LUX, SILK and cosmetics were kept. canon

How the world explains it

ONI told Runners that the fiscal season was over: credits and gear went toward their debt, and Cradle and faction upgrades were rolled back. canon

The only new piece of lore delivery between July and October. It is the same debt CyberAcme uses to recruit Runners through NextForm Recourse (file 07). theory ours

What else changed

  • A free NuCaloric Reward Pass.
  • Sponsored Queues on weekends, Thu-Mon; the first was Sponsored Perimeter, October 8-12.
  • More XP for fighting the UESC, lower faction reputation requirements.
  • Solo: the UESC is less aggressive and deals 20% less damage to Runners.
  • Repeater HPR and Hardline PR reworked; DRRVSH and Magnum nerfed; Recon's Echo Pulse nerfed in PvP. The available notes give no exact numbers.
  • Vault Breaker's return announced: October 20 - November 9.

10.10

Cradle

Season 2 // progressionThe cradle of your body

The main system of the second season, with better lore underneath than it first seems. In the game, Cradle is described as routing energy to WEAVEworm colonies to retune a shell's stats. Remember file 05: the worms that wove your body are always hungry, and Cradle is where you feed them. In the fiction, a spare gun handed to the Matter Converter goes to the worms, and the colony reworks your biomatter harder and faster. Levelling up here is feeding time. The top perk of the Dexterity branch is called WEAVEworms. canon theory

In practice

You earn energy two ways: by completing contracts, or by opening the Vault and feeding spare loot to the Matter Converter (bulk select was added so you can clear out junk in a minute after a session; better items give more). Energy goes into six stat branches, Strength, Recharge, Dexterity, Endurance, Support, Resistance, each with milestone nodes and a signature perk at the top (Leech for Strength, faster abilities for Recharge, revive perks such as Factory Reset for Support). The whole tree is shared by all your shells, a respec is free and instant and you can do it as often as you like, and the full cap is Cradle level 84. canon

Community advice

The community says to start with Endurance: heat capacity works as stamina here, and Marathon is a game about positioning. The cheap Quick Vent and Heat Dissipation nodes let you play more aggressively from day one. After that, tune the branches to your shell; points spread evenly give you a "sandwich" build that is mediocre at everything. Save up for a node: energy stuck halfway to a milestone does nothing until you top it up. datamine

Does Cradle survive a wipe

It does not. Bungie's wording, Cradle Evolution on July 21 and Nightfall Refresh itself cleared up the June confusion: on October 6 Cradle levels were reset along with Runner and faction levels, while LUX, SILK and cosmetics were kept. Bungie promises a Cradle rework by March 2027. canon
