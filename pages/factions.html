FILE 07 // INFLUENCE IS POWER // 6 FACTIONS

UESC CORRIDOR // BUNGIE PRESS KIT, DECEMBER 2025

Factions

Six corporations and groups hire Runners, and each one has its own AI agent handing you contracts. Everyone starts with CyberAcme: its six introductory contracts unlock the rest. Each agent is listed with a voice actor; the full cast, about 33 people, was revealed in January 2026. canon

NEW RUNNER > start with ONI from CyberAcme: her six introductory contracts unlock the other five factions. Since the October 6 reset the reputation thresholds are lower, so getting in takes less time than it did at launch.

CURATOR>File 07. Six employers. Read them in order.

07.1

Contracts

How the work goesThe loop

You take a contract from a faction's agent, do it during a raid and turn it in when you get back. The pay is reputation with that faction plus rewards. As reputation grows, so does the faction vendor's stock: better shells, weapons and mods. Then round again, with harder contracts. canon

Oct 6 reset

1.1.9.2Nightfall Refresh wiped faction levels along with Runner level, Cradle, Vault and zone access; LUX, SILK and cosmetics stayed. In exchange, faction reputation requirements went down and fighting the UESC pays more XP: you run the loop again, only faster. canon

07.2

CyberAcme

Agent ONI // voice: Donnla HughesBroker

"Everything runs on CyberAcme. Or nothing runs at all." The digital infrastructure of all humanity, and the neutral broker every Runner contract passes through.

Who they are

CyberAcme doesn't fight for the planet. It sells "hardware and software for all applications" to all six sides and takes a cut of every contract. Its ONI (Onboard Navigational Intelligence; Halo's spy agency has the same name, and Bungie knows it) is a floating digitized woman's head and your first guide. ONI is also a product: the firmware runs in nearly every Runner shell. Most of the Marathon's own systems were CyberAcme's as well ("the majority of the UESC MARATHON's systems were powered by CYAC"), so on Tau Ceti IV the company is walking back into its own legacy. On the original ship it ran most of the onboard computer systems; today it works as a neutral broker and keeps the infrastructure running. Hence the starting-faction role: the whole tech stack a Runner stands on, from the ONI firmware in the shell to the contract network, belongs to CyberAcme, and ONI support walks new Runners through their first contracts. canon

Underneath

Behind the businesslike front there is more than rumour: the wiki states outright that the corporation "deals in the illegal buying and selling of personality and thought data". The Runner contract gives it co-ownership of the copies of your mind, so you are sold before your first raid. It recruits through the NextForm Recourse program, buying up a candidate's debts and turning them into a decorporealized debtor: "This debt has been purchased by CyberAcme and added to your balance". canon

Allies and enemies

On paper CyberAcme is neutral. In practice it brokers contracts for every side and is tied into partnerships (Cradle with Sekiguchi, supplies for Traxus). It holds the UESC in cold contempt; according to one press source ONI calls the destruction of the government "completely acceptable", though that line has not been matched word for word in the game. canon theory

What it gives a Runner

A soft onboarding: shell upgrades, shorter cooldowns, more inventory. Its six contracts open the other five factions. ONI always signs off with the same gentle line: "Congratulations, and sweet dreams". canon datamine

1.1.9Update 1.1.9 (September 1, 2026) opened the C.A.R.R.I armory at CyberAcme: Superior Shield Implant and Superior Backpack, with stock rotating three times a week. CyberAcme Commendations rewards got cheaper at the same time: Deluxe Backpack from 20 to 15, Deluxe Shield Implant from 12 to 10, Deluxe Key Template from 40 to 30, Superior Key Template from 75 to 50. canon

1.1.9.2On October 6, 2026 ONI told Runners that the fiscal season was over: their credits and gear went toward their debt, and Cradle and faction upgrades were rolled back. That is how Nightfall Refresh explained the progress wipe inside the world. canon

It is the same debt CyberAcme buys up through NextForm Recourse: for the first time a wipe is framed as debt collection under the Runner contract. theory ours

07.3

NuCaloric

Agent Gaius // voice: Darin De PaulInvestigator

"Essentially. Everywhere." A monopoly on food, medicine and housing that is quietly investigating what killed the colony.

Who they are

"Feed, clothe, and supply humanity": the Codex describes the model as a mix of social good and ruthless optimization. Founded by Gus Moraine as a philanthropy during the famine of the 2360s, NuCaloric grew into the largest supplier of goods in the Solar System, from Biostock and biosynthetic pets to pharmaceuticals, housing modules and cosmetics. On Tau Ceti IV it avoids taking ground by force on principle ("does not appear interested in sustained confrontation") and prefers to gather historical data and intel through hired Runners ("hired RUNNER operatives"). It works as an investigator. NuCaloric is also listed among the corporate sponsors of the Marathon expedition that once carried the colonists to Tau Ceti, so it is investigating a story it took part in. canon

Underneath

The philanthropy comes with worse details. NuCaloric's "tick milk" is made from exactly what you think it is. Founder Gus Moraine personally recorded the welcome message for the colonists and insisted on the word "sacrifice", and an ad for the "Drinkable Cheeseburger" ran right after it. In the exclusive contract with the UESC, clause 616 locks in the lease of property supplied to the colonists for at least 600 years, and the colonists themselves are not a party to it. According to the press, Gaius knows more about the colony's death than he says: his motives are "slightly askew". canon theory

Allies and enemies

It avoids open conflict with everyone; its combat lore admits that NuCal is "not yet wholly comfortable with open conflict". It once picked the planet as the mission target together with Traxus. Under the 600-year contract to supply the colony, NuCaloric is legally tied to the fate of Tau Ceti IV, and that thread to the mystery of the colony's death has nothing to do with Traxus. canon

What it gives a Runner

Survival items: shield charges, medkits, regenerative implants. The faction's tone is set by Gaius's welcome to the colonists: "Humanity thanks you for your sacrifice". canon datamine

1.1.9.2Nightfall Refresh (October 6, 2026) gave the faction a free NuCaloric Reward Pass: progress rewards with no purchase. canon

07.4

Traxus

Agent Vulcan // voice: Morla GorrondonaPuppeteer

"Operate Beyond Scale." A space-age East India Company that treats the colony and its technology as its own property, due to be handed back.

Who they are

The richest private corporation in human history, second in influence only to the UESC ("second only to the UNIFIED EARTH SPACE COUNCIL"). Founded on Earth in 2095 as Traxus Intelligent Systems to "pierce the veil of the cosmos", it moved to Mars for minerals in 2145. It works through other people's hands and through fronts: officially Traxus is "not present" on Tau Ceti IV, and it uses Runners as a deniable asset to "scavenge the colony in direct opposition of UESC forces". Legally it claims "rights of reacquisition" over all technology tied to the Marathon and the colony, which means it considers the planet its own. Agent Vulcan does no persuading. Vulcan sits motionless on a throne with glowing eyes and reads out the disclaimers under which you are a "temporary nonasset worker" with no rights at all. Right now Traxus is openly contesting salvage rights to the empty New Cascadia with the UESC: both sides treat the colony's ruins and hardware as their lawful prize. That salvage is why the corporation keeps dropping Runners onto the planet. canon

Underneath

Traxus IV, the AI that brought down Mars in 2206, was the company's own fourth-generation system; the corporation survived the scandal. The Marathon's three ship AIs (Leela, Tycho, Durandal; IX-A "Hermes" series, 2409) were also physically built by Traxus, though their rival CyberAcme administers them. And "Project Goliath" is the file whose leak stalled Traxus's talks with the UESC overnight and sent the corporation to the planet on its own (see 07.8 Network). canon

Allies and enemies

Publicly Traxus fights the UESC over salvage and "rights of reacquisition". Privately it pulls the strings: according to datamined text MIDA "had been formally brought into the fold by Traxus OffWorld Industries", and the anti-corporate revolution became a Traxus asset. Add an old pact with NuCaloric over the choice of planet, and Arachne cultists hired for salvage runs. datamine

What it gives a Runner

The most expensive loot: weapon upgrades and mods under strict corporate control. Traxus pitches even to whole countries ("Choose freedom. Let Traxus steward what you have built"), and in a live on-air argument with a UESC admiral it denies everything and closes on a veiled threat: "Our success is your success". canon datamine

1.1.5.4During the Ordnance Heist event (August 18 - September 1, 2026), UESC Manifests looted in Perimeter were accepted by two armories at once, Traxus and MIDA, even though the two factions are enemies. canon

07.5

MIDA

Agent _gantry // voice: Lee ShortenRevolution

"Resist. Disrupt. Destroy." Martian revolutionaries who dream of burning down the old world, and who were quietly bought by Traxus.

Who they are

In _gantry's plain words, the goal is to "burn away a stagnant, corrupt world" and build a new one on the far side of the revolution. MIDA is a Martian political party turned anarchist network, and its grudges are older than the Marathon. They go back to the massacre at the Misriah food distribution center on January 4, 2345, which started the Third Martian War, and the network hardened after the massacre of 2442. The acronym has no canonical expansion: the ARG deliberately offered contradictory ones, from "Martian Independence Demands Accountability" to the joke "Mars Is Damnably Arid". Agent _gantry talks in graffiti-like fragments and uploads malware to stay untraceable. The name MIDA comes from the 90s trilogy, where a Martian rebel movement against Earth's government already existed and was later crushed. Bungie then reused the name in Destiny (the MIDA Multi-Tool rifle) and has now brought it back here. The in-joke is almost 30 years old and has gone through three games. canon

Underneath

During its three months of rule over Mars in 2466, MIDA was responsible for the deaths of roughly a tenth of the planet's population. While the Marathon was still under construction, its operatives smuggled weapons and combat robots (Battleroids) aboard, hoping to start a new uprising far from Earth. The newest item comes from datamined text: MIDA "had been formally brought into the fold by Traxus OffWorld Industries", so the anti-corporate network now works for a megacorporation in all but name. The young wing calls the old guard "corrupt" for it. canon datamine

Allies and enemies

Open war with the UESC, and Traxus holding the leash from above. Datamined text also credits MIDA with leaking the "Project Goliath" file that started the corporate race for the planet (see 07.8 Network). With Arachne the network keeps a cynical temporary alliance: to the cult, MIDA's ideology is useful chaos and little more. canon datamine

What it gives a Runner

Sabotage tools: demolition weapons and EMP, contracts to blow up sites and plant malware all over Tau Ceti IV; your Vandal comes from here too. The network has its own people inside the security forces: _gantry shares a dossier on Captain Orion and suspects him of deploying an unregistered military AI, "Cerberus, capable of mass violence". canon datamine

1.1.5.4During the Ordnance Heist the MIDA armory took UESC Manifests just like the Traxus armories did: a convenient storefront for the deal the datamine describes above. canon

07.6

Arachne

Agent Charter // voice: Dave FennoyDeath cult

"See red. Leave blue." A registered death cult for which every kill is both a ritual and a line of data.

Who they are

The thermodynamics of violence: squeeze the absolute maximum out of the shell and the fight. Officially Arachne is "a registered 784s(g) spiritual group", so the death cult has a legal entity and religious status. The doctrine grew out of real failures of early cryo technology (8% of class 1-AC subjects developed severe psychoses): death here is "a recoverable form of death", and cryosleep is a path to enlightenment after death; the teaching is divided into Houses, Hands and Eyes. In the game the motive comes down to a formula: death and the data you can collect from it are one whole. canon

Underneath

The sect's warrior novices take a single vow: "Draw your blades only ever in service to sleep". Officially nobody knows where its money comes from; Charter refers to unknown "my Patrons". In the game the agent appears as a godlike figure with many arms, one of them holding the heart of a dead UESC soldier. canon

Allies and enemies

On salvage runs Arachne works as a Traxus subcontractor, and it keeps a temporary alliance with MIDA for the chaos. The planet itself means nothing to the cult; it fights for death and data. Charter and the cult are death-givers, and what interests them is Runners killing Runners. Mechanics and lore both make Arachne the PvP faction: its contracts pay for hunting other players. canon

What it gives a Runner

Pure PvP: killstreaks and hunting other Runners; according to the press the reward is specific, intel on enemy squad positions. Charter is cold and direct: "Sow war to feed entropy". canon datamine

07.7

Sekiguchi

Agent Nona // voice: Tracy WilesBioengineers

Public sources list no official slogan for Sekiguchi. Makers of shells and weaver worms, for whom the whole planet is a testing ground for the Runner body.

Who they are

Sekiguchi Genetics is only part of the larger Sekiguchi Group, and the sources say nothing about its other lines of business. WEAVEworm technology runs from 2198 (EARTHworm, built for waste disposal) through seven centuries of iterations to printing biocybernetic shells, and at its core is an engineered hunger: with a damaged program the worms "will attempt to devour one another by default". Agent Nona is a white, winged, insect-like creature with red eyes and many arms who tends the worms and asks in a soft voice: "Are we not a force for good?". This is the endurance faction: it pays for surviving the most dangerous zones and has no use for aggression. The mechanics of your immortality come from Sekiguchi as well. The shells are grown from WEAVEworm biomatter, and the neural sync and mind transfer into a fresh shell that bring you back after death are its work too. To Nona a Runner is a field test subject, handy for checking how long her shells hold up. canon

Underneath

Under Nona's soft voice sits the harshest practice on the body: the press calls Sekiguchi the most malevolent faction, the one pushing the limits of human biology. Shell printing relies on the worms cannibalizing each other. And its SIMbiont is a prototype that rival CyberAcme openly tells Runners to limit: "I recommend you limit your exposure to the SIMbiont". canon

Allies and enemies

The main knot is with CyberAcme: they build the Cradle shell printer together (Sekiguchi the hardware, CyberAcme the software), and ONI has direct access inside the partner's product. The same partner tells Runners to limit Sekiguchi's SIMbiont. canon

What it gives a Runner

Biological upgrades for endurance: less time bleeding out, shorter cooldowns; data from the most dangerous zones traded for body upgrades. Nona also tells parables about Death: "I have consumed every piece of human literature ever recorded". canon

07.8

Network

The hidden inter-corporate networkStorefront and deals

Behind the open feuds on Tau Ceti IV sit deals between the same "enemies". The Marathon's three ship AIs were physically built by Traxus (IX-A "Hermes" series, 2409), while their rival CyberAcme administers them. The Cradle shell printer is a joint product of CyberAcme (software) and Sekiguchi (hardware), and ONI has direct access inside the partner's product. The planet itself was chosen as the mission target by a joint NuCaloric and Traxus commission before the Marathon ever launched. And Traxus, publicly "not present" on the planet, keeps agents inside MIDA and hires Arachne cultists as ordinary mercenaries for salvage runs. canon

The common thread: Project Goliath

The start of the game hangs on one file. The UESC commissioned Project Goliath to restore contact with the Tau Ceti IV colony; after the United Shipping hack the file leaked (datamine pins the leak on MIDA, from a single source), and once all of Goliath was out, Traxus's talks with the UESC stalled overnight and the corporation went to the planet on its own. Then, per the datamine, MIDA "had been formally brought into the fold by Traxus OffWorld Industries": the anti-corporate revolution that once hacked Traxus correspondence became an asset of the same megacorporation. The MIDA split (the radicals call the old guard "corrupt") probably runs along the line of that deal. CyberAcme stays "above the fray" only on paper: it brokers everyone's contracts and profits from each one, while NuCaloric stays out of the fighting, quietly investigates the colony's death and knows more than it says. canon datamine theory

07.9

UESC

Not a faction // main enemyThe enemy

The UESC, Earth's government, is not on the faction list. It is the main enemy of every Runner. Its rescue and occupation corps arrived on the UESC Equanimity T-SR 151, a modified Kingfisher-class rescue ship under Captain Ningal T. Enki, crewed by clones and carrying a separate rampancy response team. Forces on the planet answer to Captain Orion (voiced by Rich Keeble), who wears an artificial body himself, same as you. He has been killed. They printed a new one. You are here illegally, and their robots and drones shoot without warning. In a raid, three sides shoot at you: UESC forces, other Runners (PvP) and the creatures of the Anomaly. canon
