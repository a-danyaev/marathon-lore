FILE 06 // DEATH DEALERS // 9 MODELS

VANDAL // BUNGIE CINEMATIC

Shells

Bungie's official tagline for shells is "Powerful. Configurable. Expendable." In the lore every playable class is a separate shell model with its own history. Looks, voice and temperament come from the personality series: one "face" printed onto thousands of identical bodies. That is why every Vandal in the game looks the same. They are a mass-market product. Nona, the agent of their maker Sekiguchi, calls each shell a marvel of biosynthetic engineering and sweetly adds that your predecessors were less lucky. canon

NEW RUNNERS > for your first runs, take a forgiving all-rounder that gets by on durability and self-healing and doesn't demand frame-perfect ability timing. Destroyer fits best: tough, self-repairing, point and shoot. Recon earns its place by helping you read the map and the enemy. Save Vandal and Assassin, with their stealth and dashes, for later, and leave Rook alone entirely. Your first hours go into maps, sound and exfil points; ability micromanagement can wait. Once you find your feet, switch to whatever shell you like.

CURATOR>File 06. Shell models. Read them in order.

06.1

Vandal

Glitch seriesCombat anarchist

A fast skirmisher and duelist for players who like aggression and constant movement. Vandal lives on speed: Amplify sharply boosts the shell, so it can dive onto a flank, finish off a wounded target and get out just as fast. The wrist-mounted Disrupt Cannon does some damage, but that is secondary. Its real job is knocking an opponent out of position: shove them, break their aim, and near the map edge drop them off a ledge. Vandal plays on initiative and has no business in a straight trade.

Strengths

  • Exceptional mobility and range control
  • Disrupt knocks enemies out of position and off ledges
  • Ideal for flanks and finishing kills

Weaknesses

  • Fragile, struggles in a straight trade
  • Weak self-healing and melee
  • Needs precise timing and positioning

How to play

Keep moving: build speed, come in from the side or the rear, and fire Disrupt at an enemy standing near an edge or when you need to break distance. Pick lone targets and leave before their crew shows up.

Lore

The single face of the Glitch series was the whole point: the authorities could not track individual dissidents. The wardrobe borrows from the counterculture of the Quincy and Palace Mons districts, and Glitch streetwear is still in fashion among Martian youth as a sign of resistance.

According to the in-game Runners' Guide, Amplify grew out of a hack, and the guide does not count it as a Vandal feature. Shell athletes found the boost technique first by jailbreaking the shell's movement mechanics; MIDA weaponised it and started the modern wave of boost abilities that Runners of every faction now use. Vandals themselves, the guide says, move at speeds that would break another shell's biosynthetic bones. The Disrupt Cannon on the forearm is a converted energy cannon, stripped down and grafted into a cyber-arm. It knocks opponents out of position and sometimes off a ledge. canon

Details

The series' former name is "Vandal (Formerly Glitch)": in 2025 the shell was shown under its old name. datamine

The lore presents the debut raid as a newsfeed headline, "A Dozen Lunatics Bumrush UESC Mobile Armory"; to quote the source exactly, MIDA recruitment grew "7500%", the ×75 figure. The series motto is about making illegal running fashionable: it is easier to disappear when a whole subculture grows up around your look. Disrupt Cannon flavour text: "Nothing better than a trick up your sleeve". Vandal is voiced by Erica Lindbeck. canon

CHANNEL 06.2SEASON 2 // COLOUR UNDER THE LENS

06.2

Destroyer

Locus seriesAssault trooper

A heavy assault shell and the most forgiving pick for a new player: durable, self-repairing, point and shoot. The Prime module Search and Destroy is shoulder-mounted homing artillery. Keep an enemy in your sights and every third hit calls a micro-missile salvo onto the same spot, which turns steady fire into heavy damage. The tactical Riot Barricade throws up a barrier you can rest, reload or block a passage behind. Destroyer holds the front line and pushes positions by brute force.

Strengths

  • High durability and self-repair
  • Strong sustained damage, missile salvos for accurate fire
  • The barrier gives cover and holds a passage

Weaknesses

  • Slow and heavy, hard to reposition
  • Conspicuous and easy to read
  • Long Prime cooldown, no stealth

How to play

Take the front line, drop the barrier in narrow passages and keep firing so the missile salvos trigger. Self-repair lets you trade damage aggressively: press the advantage, don't back off.

Lore

Destroyer is a product of UESC Combat Science Labs. Since the "Consequence of Violence" amendment (2879) its military use has been officially banned, but corporations still run it in private security. The Locus personality series was trained on the combat experience of UESC soldiers and gives its wearer a steady calm and confidence in a fight, whatever the threat level. canon

Brutality was not the only reason Destroyer was pulled from service. The Prime module Search and Destroy, shoulder-mounted homing artillery where every third round that lands calls a micro-missile salvo onto the point of impact, proved too effective, and soldiers who relied on its neural HUD targeting for long periods showed psychological destabilisation. Runners have been getting hold of the model through black-market channels since at least 2872. canon

Details

The series' former name is "Destroyer (Formerly Locus)". datamine

The Locus personality was modelled on field commanders. Destroyer is voiced by JB Tadena; in the launch cinematic an aggressive Destroyer ends up making peace between a Triage and a Thief who have fallen out. canon

Rumour has it the official reason for the ban covered for something worse, and that the UESC itself quietly runs Destroyers against Runners across the Sol system. theory

06.3

Assassin

Void seriesGhost

A stealth infiltrator for patient hunters. Active Camo has no button of its own: the shell goes invisible when it steps into its own smoke from the Prime module Smoke Screen. The Shadow Dive dash gets it into a fight or out of one. The job is to close in unseen, take out a lone target and vanish. In an open firefight Assassin loses.

Strengths

  • Invisibility through smoke for ambushes and escapes
  • Heavy opening strike on a lone target
  • Smoke and dash give control of the fight

Weaknesses

  • Fragile, loses drawn-out fights
  • Invisibility breaks on aggressive actions
  • High skill floor, punished when exposed

How to play

Drop smoke and step into it to go invisible, flank the enemy and hit an isolated target first. Don't linger in the open: Shadow Dive is for getting in and getting out.

Lore

The UESC has never officially admitted developing the Assassin's stealth tech, but Runners trace it back to UESC Spec Ops prototypes. Void is considered the first personality series for a shell. Legend says it is named after a nameless Runner who lost their mind in the first trials of active camouflage merged with neural transference. canon

Per the in-game guide, the Assassin's technique hinges on a combo. Active Camo is a passive trigger: nano-triggers in the chassis switch it on when the shell enters its own smoke. Smoke Screen itself comes from pocket synth-emitter discs, first built for emergency response services. The guide says the Void series turned up in archived neural code and was never officially released; it was modelled on one of the first test subjects to die, the one who perfected the technology that destroyed them. canon

The rumour puts the consciousness transfer more harshly than the guide does: it caused "near total memory loss, followed by complete loss of self". Sources do not reproduce the mention of classified "UESC/SEKGEN files" word for word, so that detail is unconfirmed. theory

Details

The series' former name is "Assassin (Formerly Void)". datamine

Series motto: the invisible blade strikes first. Void is voiced by Elias Toufexis, who also voices Rook. In a cinematic Blackbird asks "Do you ever wonder what happened to these people?" and Void answers flatly, "It's not my business" (sources disagree on which video the line comes from). canon

CHANNEL 06.4UESC ORBIT // COLOUR UNDER THE LENS

06.4

Recon

Blackbird seriesScout

A scout that wins on information, with shooting a distant second. The Prime module Echo Pulse sends out a ping wave that lights up nearby enemies, and the tactical Tracker Drone works like a living grenade: it seeks out a threat, chases it and detonates, flushing out anyone dug in. Recon helps the whole crew read the map and see the enemy coming, and helps a new player learn the zones and enemy habits faster.

Strengths

  • Best scouting in the game, the ping lights up enemies
  • The drone hunts down and flushes out campers
  • Helps the crew and new players read the map

Weaknesses

  • Weak direct damage and melee
  • Helpless in a fair duel
  • Its value depends on whether the crew uses its intel

How to play

Ping before entering a new area, send the drone in to flush out campers, and play for the crew: they need you for intel, so leave the trading to others. Stay out of duels and keep behind your teammates.

1.1.9.2The Nightfall Refresh update (October 6, 2026) nerfed Recon's Echo Pulse in PvP. The patch notes available to us give no exact numbers. The ping is still the shell's main tool, but lean on it more carefully in duels against Runners. canon

Lore

Recon is another trophy of the UESC combat programme. Official use was shut down once the intel feed started damaging the wearer's neural integrity. The Blackbird series was built to keep soldiers psychologically stable under fire, and Runners cracked a hidden "command:follow" protocol inside it, hard-coded into every combat personality matrix from UESC Combat Science Labs. canon

The guide explains how the mind damage worked: the flow of incoming intel could scramble the neural integrity of the mind housed inside, which is why the UESC closed the programme. Jailbreaking the hard-coded "command:follow" raised an awkward question about whether a Runner is autonomous at all, and that pushed the UESC to wind down public Runner operations. The Blackbird personality was designed as a command presence with an emphasis on directness, so that intel arrives undistorted. canon

Details

The series' former name is "Recon (Formerly Blackbird)". datamine

The guide describes the Tracker Drone as a living grenade stripped of all biology and sentience, left with nothing but threat assessment and an urge to detonate. Besides the known abilities, the series lists the traits Interrogation and Stalker Protocol. Voiced by Nika Futterman. canon

06.5

Thief

Icon seriesLoot runner

A thief built around raid economy and mobility. The Grapple Device is an anti-grav maglock disc on a cable that pulls the shell toward a target: use it to reach high ground, close distance fast or break off a fight. The Pickpocket Drone cleans out a target, and the X-Ray Visor sees through walls to show loot and enemies. Thief wins by grabbing the valuables faster than anyone and leaving with them; head-on fights are not its game.

Strengths

  • Fastest looting in the game
  • The grapple gives mobility, high ground and a way out
  • Visor and drone expose loot and enemies

Weaknesses

  • Weak in head-on fights, lower durability
  • Strength lies in positioning and looting; damage is low
  • Greed for loot gets punished fast

How to play

Grapple up to high ground and stashes, scan areas with the visor before going in, loot fast and exfil. Don't get greedy in firefights: Thief is good at looting and escaping and loses straight trades.

Lore

Rumour says the first Thief was a Sekiguchi in-house build for a raid on a rival. The hired Runner never returned the shell, and a few months later the specs leaked onto the black markets. The Icon series is fully aftermarket: it copies the face and voice of Sekiguchi's former lead Shell Science Director, Dr. Yoki Samura, which gives Thief a recognisable public image instead of anonymity. canon

The Thief leak turned into a high-profile case: in late 2885 the UESC opened an investigation into SEKGEN, right as the TRAX data breach was unfolding. The Icon series rests on a paradox, since Thieves value showmanship and anonymity at once, two opposite traits. Dr. Yoki Samura's face gives law enforcement zero insight into the real identity and motives of the wearer and at the same time turns the shell into a recognisable brand. The shell itself is described outright as custom-built to take the prize. canon

Details

The series' former name is "Thief (Formerly Icon)". datamine

The series' abilities all carry thieves' names: Grapple Device, Pickpocket Drone, X-Ray Visor and The Finer Things. The Grapple Device is a maglock anti-grav disc on a reinforced flex cable that pulls the shell toward a target; the guide calls it one more example of Runner ingenuity. canon

CHANNEL 06.6TRIAGE // COLOUR UNDER THE LENS

06.6

Triage

Aux seriesField medic

A field medic and the backbone of a crew. The Prime module Reboot+ is what the guide calls the ideal healing weapon: aim at a downed teammate and they get up from a distance; aim at an enemy and you hit them with an EMP. The tactical Med-Drone heals allies mid-fight. Reviving a teammate with the Mercy Kit briefly cloaks the shell, covering a risky revive. Triage keeps the crew on its feet, and UESC forces hate field revives more than anything.

Strengths

  • Remote revives and healing keep the crew in the fight
  • EMP suppresses enemy shields and tech
  • Strong self-repair, the cloak covers revives

Weaknesses

  • Low personal damage and mobility
  • A priority target for the enemy
  • Ineffective alone, without a crew

How to play

Stay close to the crew but in cover: your priority is picking up the fallen and healing with the drone. Use the cloak window to revive safely and hit shielded enemies with EMP. Don't play hero on your own.

Lore

Traxus built Triage for the Martian mines, where deadly conditions were burning through worker shells and a medic was meant to keep them on the job. The Aux personality series was developed together with the UESC and Sekiguchi Genetics before the official debut; Runners got to it before the market did and retuned its calm "medical" voice for combat aggression. According to the wiki (citing the game), Triage models saw heavy use in the corporate cold war on Tau Ceti IV in 2893. canon (thanks to u/URTISK for the tip)

In the guide's words, the Prime module Reboot+ is the ideal healing weapon: through its defib actuators it either revives a downed teammate or fires an EMP at an enemy, depending on where you aim. It started out as emergency response gear that Runners turned into a lifeline and a weapon for hostile zones. The design idea is a medic who cannot rely on others because others rely on them. The stock Aux voice brings a soothing bedside manner, and Runners added combat aggression on top. canon

Details

The cinematic "In Death We've Just Begun" was directed by Harmony Korine and Sam Goldwater, with music by Ryan Lott (Son Lux) and vocals and lyrics by Poppy. Triage is not alone on screen: a Triage, Thief and Destroyer crew reaches the endgame Cryo Archive aboard the Marathon, where some entity attacks them; the Thief runs off with the loot and the Triage drags the Destroyer out. Voiced by Elliot Knight. canon

06.7

Sentinel

Rampart series // Season 2Defensive engineer

A defensive engineer for holding positions and controlling ground, new in Season 2. Its Prime is the Defender System, a deployable platform that shoots down incoming grenades and rockets and steadies the weapons of nearby allies. Bullets pass straight through; explosives and even recon drones get stopped. The tactical Snare Mine is a proximity mine that immobilises everyone in its radius. The passive Castle Doctrine hardens the shell when enemies get close. Sentinel is at its best where you need to dig in and make the enemy come at a prepared defence.

Strengths

  • Neutralises grenades, rockets and recon drones, steadies allies' aim
  • The mine locks down passages and approaches
  • Castle Doctrine holds up up close, ideal for holding points

Weaknesses

  • Static, weak when forced to push or rotate fast
  • The platform doesn't stop bullets
  • The defence takes time to set up

How to play

Put the platform on objectives and exfil points to neutralise explosives and buff the crew, mine the approaches and keep fights close under Castle Doctrine. Let the enemy come to your position.

Lore

Sentinel's first recorded appearance is the Trenchway Holdout siege under Antarctica. A crew of radical scientists in stolen UESC shells held out for three months; the Central Security Agency lost 27 agents and in the end collapsed the tunnels. Once the Trenchway tactics leaked into the Runner scene, Sentinel became the standard for scouting and sorties. In Sol the GADGE (Rampart) personality series is officially classed as illegal identity theft: it was compiled from stolen neural models of mechanical engineers at Intelligent Design Labs. canon

The Defender System shoots down anything explosive (grenades, rockets) and lets bullets through. Originally military hardware, it is tuned against Runner munitions and over time learned to counter shell threats such as the Destroyer's Search and Destroy and recon drones. The tactical Snare Mine is a hand-thrown detonating device with a proximity sensor that scatters submunitions to immobilise everyone in the radius; it began life as mining explosives scaled down to anti-personnel size. canon

Details

Sentinel is probably the first shell ever shown to the public: per the wiki it opened the original Announcement Trailer (a single stub source, so the check is incomplete), although as a class it only arrived in Season 2 (June 2, 2026). A damaged Sentinel helmet with UESC combat logs turns up as loot aboard the Marathon. canon

CHANNEL 06.8NIGHT MARSH // COLOUR UNDER THE LENS

06.8

Rook

No series // robo-frameScavenger

A last-resort mode for experienced players, with little in common with a regular class: a solo drop into a match already under way, random junk instead of gear, contracts locked and no cosmetics. The Prime module Recuperation simply triggers the frame's factory emergency repair system to buy a little more time. The tactical Signal Mask is a holo-mask that fools autonomous UESC combat robots: they take Rook for one of their own until it opens fire or breaks into a sprint. The bet is that a skilled player beats a well-stocked one. Rook is not for new players.

Strengths

  • Signal Mask gets you past UESC robots
  • Recuperation buys extra durability
  • A pure skill test with nothing to lose

Weaknesses

  • No stats, no implants, random loadout
  • One against three, contracts paused
  • A brutally punishing mode, not for beginners

How to play

Avoid open fights against three, use the mask to pass through zones with UESC robots, pick up better gear off the ground and go for isolated targets. Skill and map knowledge carry you here; the loadout matters little.

Lore

Rook is a hijacked baseline robotic frame with no biomatter, the kind the UESC and the corporations use for manual labour. Runners fit it with a temporary neural sync adapter and occupy the body until neural degradation sets in. No implants, no cores: Rook never reaches the full functionality of a biocybernetic shell. The tagline "Lunar work release" hints at roots in lunar penal labour. canon

Rook's abilities are entirely Runner-made. Recuperation just taps the frame's emergency repair system, fitted at the factory so the machine stays usable after industrial accidents, and lets you hang on a little longer on the scrapheap. Signal Mask is a pure back-channel hack put together somewhere in Tau Ceti orbit as an answer to the UESC's mobile war crimes. The holo-mask fools the sensors of autonomous UESC combat robots (ARS), and they take Rook for one of their own until it opens fire or breaks into a sprint. Nobody knows who wrote it: the specs were simply dropped into a local back-channel and spread like wildfire. canon

Details

Rook's in-game description is a wall of warnings that ends in "GOOD LUCK": solo only, limited loadout, contracts paused, restricted zone access, Season Level 3 required. Rook has no cosmetics at all, and every stat in the table is zero, the only class like that. Rook is voiced by Elias Toufexis, the same actor who voices Assassin (Void). canon

06.9

Bishop

Symbiosis // DEC 8, 2026Coming Dec 8
Channel 06.9NO SIGNALDossier opens Dec 8

"High-performance robot samurai" is how Bungie introduces the new shell. A fast, surgical hunter-killer: speed, a sudden entry and burst damage up close. canon

What we know

It arrives with the free Symbiosis update on December 8, 2026, the same one that brings the first permanent PvE and an expanded Perimeter. Abilities, personality series and voice actor have not been announced yet. canon

The new shell was first promised for Season 3 on September 22; on September 24 Bungie dropped the fixed season cycle and moved it into Symbiosis. canon

The archive's bet

Bishop will be the first shell whose main ability deals no ranged damage. theory ours

Forecast in FORECAST.LOG, resolves December 8, 2026.

Archive footnote // a legal detail

The 2879 Destroyer ban and the "Consequence of Violence" amendment describe one event: a police unit proved so brutal that shells were banned from military use across the whole system. Every shell dropping onto Tau Ceti today has outlived its own ban.
