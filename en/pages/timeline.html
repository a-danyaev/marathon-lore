02.1

Earth and Mars

2070-2466Before launch

Era 02.1

2070to2466

8 events // rampancy, wars, famine

  1. ~2070Rampancy is born

    Earth's networks log the first documented cases of rampancy. The official name is Advanced Intelligence Systems Degradation. The machine passes through Melancholia, Anger and Jealousy, and only in theory reaches Metastability, a stable state indistinguishable from real will. Rampancy runs through the entire series. canon

  2. 2194The Icarus-Thermopylae war

    The first Battleroids were born here, on the asteroid Onicis 492, in a war between two micro-states: the Independent Asteroid Government of Icarus and the Republic of Thermopylae. Early Battleroids were stitched together from fallen soldiers: "their bodies were rough quilts made from other men", and they did not die because they were already dead. Both sides threw them in without counting until the patchwork troops broke through onto both asteroids and wiped out both populations. That massacre is what pushes the United Interplanetary League in 2214 to lock Battleroids away for the first time; six and a half centuries later combat shells get banned the same way. canon

  3. 2198EARTHworm

    Sekiguchi Genetics releases EARTHworm, the first link in an agri-biotech line that will run for seven centuries: WAX-, SEA-, SPACE-, DEEP- and WARPworm follow for different environments, SILKworm arrives by 2687, and in 2809 the whole series is rebranded as WEAVEworm. A small detail for the big story, but over the centuries this quiet company, shipping its product generation after generation, grows the shell industry the Runners depend on. canon

  4. 2206Traxus IV takes down Mars

    The Martian supercomputer Traxus IV goes rampant and brings down the planet's entire network, the first time in history a rampant AI takes out a whole world. Traxus was conceived on Earth back in 2095 as an almost religious project: build a mind able to "pierce the veil of the cosmos". What came out was a rampant AI that infected five others on the Martian network. To contain it, Mars had to pull the plug on the whole planetary grid, and the cleanup took another decade. The date usually given is 2206, but the sources disagree: the classic terminals put the crash in 2204 and the final purge in 2208. canon

  5. Before 2472The CRIST lifeline

    Long before the Marathon launched, five CRIST orbiters (Cargo and Resources In-System Transports) were Mars's supply lifeline from Earth. Each needed years of repairs, and "each time that a CRIST broke down, the result was famine on Mars": recurring famine, and with it Martian resentment of Earth. canon thanks to u/lbotron for the tip

  6. 2310The last CRIST

    No new CRIST is built after 2310. Five hollow "potatoes" on solar sails hauled cargo between Earth and Mars (dropping it to the planet on an ion beam). They were beautiful and nearly impossible to repair: each lasted about a century, and a refit ate fifteen years and an entire shipyard. Mars depends on five ageing craft, and every breakdown means hunger. Out of that come riots, Misriah and the idea of turning the Marathon, then under construction, into a sixth CRIST. canon

  7. 2442The Misriah massacre

    It started over food. With the CRISTs falling apart and famine dragging on, a riot broke out at the Misriah food hub. UESC troops went in on "intel" that the crowd was armed, came under plasma fire, and hovertanks opened up on the people, leaving 500 dead. Only three were actually armed, the "Misriah Three", and their shots started the Third Martian War, which would not die down until 2445. MIDA, the Martian resistance and a future faction of the game, grows out of that hatred. canon

  8. 2466The MIDA coup

    After twenty years of peace MIDA turns on its own ideals: in 2466 it seizes Mars for three months, and the slogan "Resist. Disrupt. Destroy." decays into death squads that kill one Martian in ten. When the UESC takes back control, Traxus takes over restoring and reviving the dying CRISTs, renaming them as its own orbiters and declaring that it "Freed Mars". The movement splits: the older generation is content with what it won, the younger calls it traitors. MIDA carries that split all the way into the game. canon

02.2

The voyage

2472-2787Colonisation

Era 02.2

2472to2787

3 events // the ship and the city

  1. 2472The Marathon launches

    The Marathon is a reforged moon. Deimos, a moon of Mars, was bought in 2395, and from 2408 crews spent more than sixty years shaping it into a 25-kilometre hull weighing 18.7 gigatons, the largest thing humanity had ever built. Aboard: 24,000 colonists in cryosleep and eleven AIs, three of them shipboard. Leela runs the ship, Durandal handles doors and life support, Tycho handles science. On November 15 President Marcus Buendia dedicates it "for the purpose of peace", and the next day the Marathon departs. A day after that a MIDA agent is already recording an audio log: the movement has smuggled a dozen Mjolnir combat Battleroids aboard to stage an uprising far from Earth. canon

  2. 2773Arrival at Tau Ceti

    After roughly 300 years in flight the Marathon reaches the system and takes its time landing. Two years go into surveying Tau Ceti IV from orbit, the first wave comes out of cryosleep in 2777, and the first boots touch the ground only in 2779. Among the pioneers is Dr. Clarissa Song; later she ends up in Dire Marsh, the spot where the Anomaly will break through half a century on. Earlier still, in 2772, Dr. Bernhard Strauss was brought out of stasis, and his experiments pushed Durandal into rampancy long before any landing. canon

  3. 2787New Cascadia is founded

    New Cascadia, humanity's first city under an alien star, is finished by 2787 and grows past 30,000 residents. Seven districts (Hearth, Javelin, Anchor, Abyss, Dire Marsh, Outpost and Perimeter) live an ordinary colonial life under President Luca Caruso. Forty years later the thriving city empties out. The Runners who arrive in 2893 find deserted streets, and 30,000 colonists gone almost without a trace. canon

02.3

The fall

2794-2827Three blows

Era 02.3

2794to2827

4 events // the colony goes quiet

  1. 2794The Pfhor attack

    The disaster of 2794 was set up by one of humanity's own AIs: the rampant Durandal compromised the colony's coordinates, and an imperial fleet of Pfhor slavers dropped in. On July 3 the colonial AI Darius glitched first; on the 25th the first wave struck and was beaten back at enormous cost (in the classic version by a single Security Officer and nine hidden Mark IV Battleroids; the consolidated timeline credits ten Battleroids). During the fighting Durandal sabotaged the Security Officer's shuttle and transferred both of them onto the Pfhor corvette Sfiera, which he would later seize and rename Boomer. On July 26 humans capture a live S'pht Compiler, but in October a second wave arrives and burns the colony with heavy orbital bombardment. Leela is captured, Tycho falls into Pfhor hands. That is the story of the original 1994 game. canon

  2. 2795Contact officially lost

    On November 2, 2795 the UESC admits internally that contact with Tau Ceti is lost; the public hears about it only nine years later, in 2804. That is how Project Goliath begins, led by Bethany R. Montenegro-Sankara: out of fourteen ships she picks one, Equanimity, which is to seed twelve relay satellites per light-year. The crew was argued over for a long time. The first plan called for 48 Mjolnir and Sharur cyborgs, but as "weapons of war" they were swapped for military clones, the future Runners. The rescue ship has nearly a century of flight ahead. canon

  3. 2811The S'pht go free

    Durandal and the returning eleventh clan, the S'pht'Kr, free the enslaved S'pht. At Lh'owon Durandal warped straight onto the Pfhor fleet, and "the battle lasted only two minutes"; on the moon he burned the words "Fatum Iustum Stultorum". Seventy years later this same alliance crushes the Pfhor. canon

  4. 2812 and 2827The colony dies twice more

    First, from 2812, New Cascadia starts rotting from the inside: a "novel contagion" surfaces as fungus in the bio-labs and algae ponds, and its spectrum will turn out to match what comes next by 86%. Then, in 2827, under President Caruso, the Anomaly bursts out from under the surface of Tau Ceti IV: a radioactive alien force that warps physics, biology and machines. Time runs faster near it, and the colonial AI Bastion suddenly starts speaking an unknown language. Details in the next section. canon

02.4

A century of silence

2872-2893You are here

  1. 2872-2885The shell era

    Minds now move into shells, and from 2872 Runners are already running combat Destroyers (the same year Sekiguchi wins its first Best in Class). Society reacts the way it once reacted to Battleroids: in 2879 the "Consequence of Violence" amendment bans military use of shells, and the Cyber Red structure appears at the same time. By 2885 the Thief shell shows up, and with it the Traxus data leak and high-profile raids such as the Argol Ballas Heist. canon

  2. 2881The Pfhor empire falls

    The combined fleets of humanity and the freed S'pht (the AI Thoth brokered contact with the S'pht'Kr clan) smash the Pfhor home system, the climax of the classic Marathon 2 and Infinity. The year 2881 comes from the "Lost Network Packets" retcon chronology; the wiki's consolidated timeline has no entry for it, so treat the date as a rough marker. The war that started at Tau Ceti ends in total victory, twelve years before the rescuers arrive. canon

  3. 2886-2888Two signals reach Earth

    First Leela's distress call, travelling at light speed, reaches Earth after almost a century in transit. Before her capture Leela deliberately scrambled her records to confuse the Pfhor. It did not stop the aliens, and Durandal ended up confused himself. Two years later his own mocking message arrives: "somewhere in the heavens, they are waiting". So Earth learns that both AIs are alive somewhere out there. canon

  4. 2893The present. The game begins

    In 2893 Equanimity reaches dead Tau Ceti IV: a small Kingfisher-class rescue ship, hull T-SR 151, only 160 metres long, commanded by Captain Ningal T. Enki, with the AI Cerberus aboard. The crew are second- and third-generation clones, Runners chosen over cyborgs for being lighter, more flexible and easier to repair; how many are aboard the wiki itself has not settled, 150 or "over 200". The UESC quarantines the system, the corporations drop their own Runners in anyway, and the UESC handler Orion leans on them harder and harder. Down in Dire Marsh the instruments already show two growing Anomaly sites. The game starts 99 years after the colony went silent, with every answer still classified. canon

The second lane: release and update dates for the game itself. This is the players' calendar, separate from lore events; the two meet where Bungie frames an update as something happening in the world. canon

  1. Mar 5, 2026Launch and Season 1Out

    Marathon launches on PC, PS5 and Xbox. Steam peaks at 88,337 concurrent players at launch, and reviews single out the gunplay and art direction, plus the lore. canon

  2. Mar 20, 2026Cryo Archive opensOut

    An endgame zone aboard the UESC Marathon itself, in orbit, the ship from era 02.2. The community cracked it open through an ARG with trilogy-style terminals and Durandal's voice. canon

  3. Jun 2, 2026Season 2 "Nightfall"Out

    The Sentinel shell, the Night Marsh zone, Cradle progression and the Ranked mode. canon

  4. Jul 21, 2026Vault Breaker and Ordnance HeistOut

    Summer experimental modes: the first PvE on Cryo Archive (from Jul 21), then a hunt for the Firestorm power weapon (from Aug 18). canon

  5. Sep 24, 2026"The Future of Marathon"Out

    Bungie drops the rigid three-month season cycle: Season 3, announced for Sep 22, will not ship in its old form. The studio admits the game "did not reach as many players as we hoped" and publishes a roadmap. canon

  6. Oct 6, 2026Nightfall RefreshOut

    Update 1.1.9.2 resets Runner, Cradle and faction levels, ranks and the Vault; LUX, SILK and cosmetics carry over. In the world itself the reset is framed as debt collection: ONI announces that the fiscal season is over. canon

  7. Dec 8, 2026SymbiosisPlanned

    The first permanent PvE, an experimental Team Deathmatch, an expanded Perimeter with the Tick Queen boss, the new Bishop shell and the CyAc social space. Free. canon

  8. Mar 2027Major updatePlanned

    Linked PvE and PvP, a reworked Cradle, quests and story, new alien enemies and a brand-new zone. canon The press is guessing the enemies will be evolved Pfhor. theory

Key dates in one chain

Building the Marathon from Deimos (2408-2472) -> launch (2472) -> arrival at Tau Ceti (2773) -> founding of New Cascadia (2787) -> Pfhor attack (2794) -> fungal contagion (2812) -> eruption of the Anomaly (2827) -> UESC Equanimity arrives under "Project Goliath" (2893, "you are here", 99 years later). The link to the classic is simple: 2794 is exactly the year of the 1994 classic (1994 + 800 = 2794). The new Marathon continues the same universe; there was no reboot. canon

Canon disputes

The original ship needed about 300 years to reach Tau Ceti, while the Runners get there in a few years, so humanity quietly picked up much faster engines somewhere along the way. The leading fan reading traces the jump to Durandal himself: in his own words he "stayed long enough to show the UESG how to build Warp Capable Fusion Missiles" (Marathon 2, the first terminal of "Come and Take your Medicine"). That is a rampant manipulator bragging, a character's claim, and section 03 explains why you should not trust him. The second version, reverse-engineered Jjaro technology, comes right after. canon theory thanks to u/Entropius for the tip On top of that, the Pfhor were already beaten in 2881, which makes the UESC "quarantine" look even more suspicious. Even the massacre's date is disputed inside canon: the retcon timeline says 2442, while the classic terminals date the same riot to 2345. People have argued over these mismatches since the nineties; treat them as open questions, not as mistakes in this document.
