RUNNER IN BIOMATTER // BUNGIE PRESS KIT

Runners

You are a Runner. You gave up your human body at some point, usually because you had no better option: medical debt, food debt, a way off a dying Earth. A corporation digitised your mind, and now it lives in shells, artificial bodies. The in-game technical term for them is biomata: biocybernetic organisms printed to order. canon

NEW HERE > die in a run and you lose your gear, not your progress. Bring what you can afford to lose, and learn to get the loot out.

05.1

Weaving

Sekiguchi GeneticsHow a shell is made

Shells are made by the Japanese biotech corporation Sekiguchi Genetics. The shell is woven. Gene-modified silkworms (type WEAVEworm; the Codex slips in a Latin joke, Bombyx mori, the real domestic silkworm) spend up to eight hours building the body in layers: synthetic organs first, then the frame, then the outer skin, all of it synth-silk. Inside sits the neural block, with biosync and memo-layers, implanted through "micro-fractional instances", and that is where your mind lives. Shell blood, by the way, is blue. canon

The worm lineage

  1. EARTHworm2198 // waste recycling

The waste-recycling EARTHworm (2198) led to the SILKworm line, and SILKworm led to WEAVEworm. Sekiguchi has collected awards from Earth institutions more than once for its emergency consciousness-ejection neural networks, and the Codex entries never let you forget it. canon

05.2

Transfer

CyberAcmeDeath and its price

Die in a run and Sekiguchi's WEAVEworms weave you a new biomata shell in about 8 hours, while CyberAcme loads you in: it evacuates your mind to a buffer, backs it up and moves the personality into a fresh neural block. On reactivation you go through a grounding ritual. An AI shows you photos and objects from your organic past and checks how much of you made the trip. The immortality comes with conditions, and you pay for it. canon

  • Cost 01

    Memory leaks

    Every transfer loses or damages some memories. That is what the grounding rituals are for: Runners slowly forget who they were. canon

  • Cost 02

    You are property

    CyberAcme repairs corrupted data from its own backups. In practice, part of your personality belongs to the corporation. canon

  • Cost 03

    Debt forever

    Shells are expensive, and a body lost in a run goes on your tab. Nobody pays it off, so a Runner stays in corporate bondage for good. canon

The debt at work

1.1.9.2Bungie framed the Nightfall Refresh progress reset (Oct 6, 2026) as the Runner's debt coming due: ONI announces that the fiscal season is over, credits and gear went to pay off the debt, and Cradle and faction upgrades were rolled back. LUX, SILK and cosmetics stayed. canon

05.3

In a run

ExtractionWhat you lose

Whatever gear you carried into the run goes to whoever kills you. The Runner, meaning your character and all of their progress, carries over between runs; only the shell body dies. A lost run hurts your wallet and leaves your progress alone. canon

Archive footnote // legal detail

An amendment to an interplanetary treaty, "Consequence of Violence", formally bans military use of shells. That is why the UESC fights on Tau Ceti with humans, clones and robots, and why the Runners' whole trade tramples on the spirit of the treaty. canon
