08.1

S'pht

Cyber raceIn the game

In-game statusCompiler aboard the Marathon

An ancient race improved by cyborgisation, first "uplifted" by machines the Jjaro left behind. For a thousand years they were slaves of the Pfhor empire, which turned them into combat units, the "Compilers": floating figures in ragged red cloaks. canon

Liberation

Durandal freed them, with an agenda of its own. The lost eleventh clan, the S'pht'Kr, helped crush the Pfhor for good in 2881. canon

Today

At least one Compiler lives on the dead Marathon as a kind of eerie space librarian: it guards whatever is left on board and aggressively removes intruders, you included. canon More in 09 // Zones, Cryo Archive.

08.2

Pfhor

Slaver empireBackstory

In-game statusDefeated, their legacy lingers on the planet

Three-eyed imperial slavers and the main villains of the classic games. They are the ones who bombed the colony in 2794. By the time of the new game their empire has been crushed by the combined fleets of humans and the S'pht, but what they left behind is scattered all over the planet. canon

A comeback

Bungie promises new alien enemies for March 2027. The press thinks they may turn out to be evolved Pfhor. theory More in 08.6.

08.3

Jjaro

PrecursorsLegends

In-game statusGone, their machines remain

A vanished super-civilisation millions of years old that could fold space and time. Nearly all the advanced technology in this universe is stolen or salvaged Jjaro legacy: S'pht cyborgisation, faster-than-light drives. canon

A guess

Possibly the insides of your shell as well. If this setting has ancient engineer-gods, the Jjaro are the only ones who fit. theory

08.4

W'rkncacnter

Entities of chaosA shadow over the story

In-game statusNot directly

Unpronounceable beings of pure chaos, older than the stars, locked inside suns and black holes and warping reality by their mere presence. Even Durandal fears them; the Jjaro built machines specifically to lock them away again. canon

Tau Ceti folklore

Colonial folklore called them formless devourers out of dreams, asleep in burning prisons. canon

Fingerprints

They don't appear directly in the new game, but the Anomaly's fingerprints (broken physics, madness, voices) look suspiciously like their work. theory

08.5

Anomaly

2827 // Night MarshA riddle older than the aliens

The force lurking in the darkness of Night Marsh broke out of the depths of Tau Ceti IV in 2827. It is far older than the familiar Pfhor and lay dormant deep in the planet long before any invasion. Now it is gathering strength. What it actually is remains the game's central mystery, and the canon gives no answer. canon

Theories

  • 01Radiation from the planet's depths that broke out of control.
  • 02Mass psychosis and shared hallucinations among the colonists.
  • 03The planet itself as a single living superorganism.
  • 04An alien mind trying to talk to humans in its own language; possibly an underground fungal mycelium network that has matured into consciousness.

The theories go round in circles and rule each other out. The game offers no single right answer yet, and the question stays open. theory
