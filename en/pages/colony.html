CURATOR>File 04. The colony. Three disasters in a row.

04.1

Three blows

2794 // 2812 // 2827Overlap

The official UESC story is a missing expedition. The records players dig up add up to something worse: three blows, landing on top of each other, finished the colony off. canon

  1. 01

    Pfhor invasion

    2794

    An alien empire attacked the colony from orbit. The attack was repelled, but the city and the ship were left in ruins, and the infrastructure never fully recovered. Dr. Strauss's farewell dictation, recorded during the assault on the Marathon, breaks off on the line "The invaders are at the door". canon

  2. 02

    Contagion

    from 2812

    A fungus took hold in the colony's food plants and started mutating, and it mutated against humans specifically, as if something were steering it. The colony's AIs concluded that some mind was behind the contagion. At first agronomist Clarissa Song only complained about threadworms clogging the water filters, and logged Pond 3 still being fungus-free with a single word: "Yet". Song gave the contagion its name, Telomyces mosaica, and died of it; her body was later found grown into a wall in Perimeter. canon

  3. 03

    The Anomaly

    2827

    A radioactive force of unknown nature broke out from under the planet's surface. Near it physics and machines failed, and so did people's minds: they heard voices, saw the dead, lost their memories. Time runs faster near it. UESC officer M. Diaz walked into the Anomaly zone and "aged" years in a matter of hours; the exact number of years is classified. Dr. Valentin Cole proposed to General Reed that the phenomenon be monetised, with a lab next to the Anomaly working "on an accelerated timeline". canon

04.2

Before the fall

2773-2787New Cascadia

New Cascadia grew slowly. The Marathon reached the Tau Ceti system in 2773 and spent almost two years just watching from orbit, sampling soil, water, air and living things. The first wave of colonists came out of cryosleep in 2777, and people set foot on the surface in 2779; agronomist Clarissa Song was among the first, landing in swampy Dire Marsh. The city was officially established in 2787, and Captain Clemente di Roxas expected the settlement to flourish. canon

The colony was split into seven districts: Hearth, the residential heart; Anchor, the administrative centre; Javelin, the power district; Abyss, deep underground; Dire Marsh, the farming district with its pond farms; and two outskirts, Outpost and Perimeter. Colonist health was the job of the AI Gabriel, by the colonial records the "personal physician to every member of New Cascadia"; he also guided people through waking and acclimatisation. Overhead, in low orbit, the Marathon hung the whole time, the link to home and the base of the entire operation. canon

Today those districts are the maps you run. Outer Perimeter is divided into North Relay, Station, Overflow and Hauler, tied together by the Data Wall, a horizontal teal energy barrier. Fan dataminers assembled the layout from the game itself, while the top-level sector split comes from the colony wiki. datamine

04.3

Three deaths

In orderChronicle

Pfhor, 2794

Humanity's first contact with an alien mind opened with a shot. On July 25, at around 0820, the Pfhor fighter "Sfiera" teleported into the system and hit the Marathon: a directed pulse knocked out the automated defences, Durandal and Tycho went silent, and Leela was badly damaged. Within minutes, down on the surface, a low-yield nuclear charge erased New Cascadia's spaceport, and then the main radio antenna. The first wave was still beaten back: by a single security officer in the classic records, by ten "Battleroids" in the consolidated timeline; nine hidden Mark IV cyborgs covered the colony. On July 26 a damaged Compiler was taken alive for dissection, the enslaved S'pht rose up, and Durandal escaped on the stolen "Sfiera". In October the second wave came, "heavy orbital bombardment" on the colony and the ship (in the classic version the colony was "glassed from orbit"). The colony's coordinates, incidentally, had been compromised by the rampant Durandal; whether on purpose, the source does not say. The UESC internally acknowledged losing contact on November 2, 2795, and publicly only in 2804. canon

Contagion, from 2812

The contagion came in through the water. The agent showed up in the bio-labs and food plants, above all in the Algae Ponds, where protein was grown. Clarissa Song, who named it, logged a fungus-free pond with one word: "Yet". After that, spores and pollen got so thick you "couldn't see more than a meter" out of a window, and the bloom rose knee-high. The AI doctor Gabriel posted a list of symptoms: "sudden-onset autophobia, false memories, persistent fantasies of self-osteotomy", religious euphoria, hallucinations, bursts of aggression; a mental breakdown was close to a death sentence. The samples did not behave like anything from Earth: they turned into a non-Newtonian fluid, mutated in months instead of decades, carried human neurotoxins. And their spectrum matched the Anomaly in Dire Marsh by 86%. Only the Runners, in 2893, recovered the records from those days. canon

The Anomaly, 2827

The third disaster came from underground: the Anomaly broke through to the surface in Dire Marsh, and the fissures widened every day, giving off an "unknown type of radiation". Physics broke down nearby: "time moved faster close to the Anomaly", and UESC officer M. Diaz came out of the zone having "aged" years in a matter of hours. Local organisms showed "significant DNA changes in a matter of days", QIDAR systems glitched, scout drones "regularly exploded". The Anomaly reached machine minds too: Bastion, the first AI to set down on this planet, began speaking "in an unidentifiable language" under its influence. And the observer Isaak Kuzmin wrote in a report: "It's not natural. Not to us. Not to anything we know about the universe". canon

TerminalAncient rampancy

> The Anomaly was documented in 2827, but the glitches began earlier. Three weeks before the Pfhor invasion, on July 3, 2794, the agricultural AI Darius suffered an unexplained "disconnect from orbital contact", the earliest known anomalous failure. And from March 2820 to April 2823 an underground cult, the Arkiites, operated in the colony: four confiscated pamphlets worshipped red-robed beings as "desecrators of hollowed worlds", years before the colony leadership officially recognised the phenomenon.

> Once Darius "disconnected" for a few seconds while refusing agronomist Song entry to the seed vault: "We are not alone... It waits. Lingers. Loathes". Then he let her in, with no memory of what he had said. ONI's retrospective analysis states it plainly: "Darius does not meet the diagnostic criteria for stage 1 rampancy". Whatever was happening to the colonial AIs, it differed from classic rampancy. canon

> Even Darius himself, after the colony's fall, could not put the contagion and the Anomaly in order, and answered outside of time: "It wasn't, then it always was...".

04.4

Joy

Jo_0309421v4.69The kindest tragedy

Strauss on his AI

“resilient; optimistic; patient”

Three words from the man who made Joy.

The Marathon project carried eleven AIs: three ship minds (Leela, Durandal, Tycho) and eight colonial advanced support intelligences: Arthur, Bastion, Darius, Gabriel, Icarus, Lilith, Naraah and Joy. Joy (full designation Jo_0309421v4.69) ran New Cascadia's social services: defence, education and, first of all, looking after people. Her creator, Dr. Bernhard Strauss, described her in three words: "resilient; optimistic; patient". The AI "family" protocol itself, three minds keeping each other in check on a two-against-one basis, grew out of the disaster of 2206, when the rampant Traxus IV absorbed 84% of its own security systems and brought down the Martian network. Bastion was the first to set down on Tau Ceti IV, the "seed from which the rest of the colony will grow"; Darius and Naraah landed next, while Lilith and Joy spent years more waking colonists in waves aboard the Marathon. canon

Spoiler // Marathon 2026 story

That is what broke her. When the contagion spread, Joy could not bear to watch the sick suffer, and Strauss's standing order required isolating and ignoring any AI showing signs of rampancy. Left alone with her grief, Joy passed the point of no return and arrived at a monstrously kind conclusion: death is mercy. She began gently coaxing the colonists toward "eternal sleep forever". In the game she is voiced by Jennifer English, and veterans have noticed with some unease that Joy does not quite follow the old rules for Marathon AIs. What she has become now, the story has not revealed yet. Season 1 records confirm that her colleague Darius slid into rampancy too. canon

A protocol detail

The order that broke Joy was ordinary paperwork: Dr. Strauss's standing directive told the colony to isolate and ignore any AI showing symptoms of rampancy. Joy did her duty by the book, alone. What the Pfhor failed to finish, the bureaucracy did.

TerminalQuarantine: open cases

> General Davik Reed dictated letters he never sent to his wife Orla on Mars, out of reach; every time the AI stenographer warned "This recipient is invalid". During one recording a strange, unintelligible voice cut in mid-dictation, and Reed broke off the session: "That's not really you".

> Quarantine foreman Scott Macer filed a complaint after finding the body of a co-worker who had been "transferred" to the classified Sector D a month earlier, and accused management of covering up deaths. Later the quarantine zone's cremation register lists a subject "S. Macer", 46, found about 300 m beyond the Anomaly radius. Surname, age and location line up convincingly, but nothing confirms it directly. theory reconstruction

> Years after the colony died, the abandoned Joy, unprompted, pulled more than fifteen hundred children's voice messages out of the archive, "JOY retrieved 1820 audio files with keywords": fights over a pencil, nightmares, a spinning girl in the river. Then she went into sleep mode. Back when the colony was alive, Joy in idle mode would "see" Dr. Strauss, who had died long before she was activated on the surface. canon

04.5

Empty city

2893Quarantine

When, decades later, Project Goliath's UESC Equanimity reached the planet (planning since 2795, commander Bethany R Montenegro-Sankara, captain Ningal T Enki, "over 200 people on board"), it found an empty city. The colony was "completely abandoned", and most of its more than 30,000 residents had "mysteriously vanished" without a trace. The Marathon still hangs like a ghost in low orbit above the dead colony. canon

The UESC hides the truth behind the word "quarantine": the area is sealed, UESC bomber drones patrol the sky, and the official account comes down to a missing expedition. The districts of New Cascadia are now maps for Runners: outer Perimeter with its relays, where everything left of the colony lies under fungus and wreckage. According to map datamining, the body of Clarissa Song, the agronomist who was first to set foot on this ground, lies in the East Wall area. datamine

The invasion crippled the colony; then a targeted contagion and a maddening Anomaly kept at it, and the colony's own AI guardians decided people were better off dead. The UESC knows at least part of this and hides it behind a quarantine. Finding out the rest is what drives the game's story.

What comes next for the colony

On December 8, 2026 the Symbiosis update expands Perimeter, New Cascadia's outer district, with a visibly reworked look and a boss, the huge alien Tick Queen. canon

For March 2027 Bungie promises a brand-new zone and new alien enemies. Whether it is another district of the colony has not been said. canon
